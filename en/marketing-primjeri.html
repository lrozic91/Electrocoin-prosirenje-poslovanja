Marketing · examples

Animated spots for attracting new clients

Two short animated spots showing how Electrocoin can speak to different types of clients. The characters are made up; the facts come from the analyses on this site: the MiCA licence, Hanfa supervision and PayCek. The spots are in Croatian.

Crypto for everyone (Kripto za svakoga)

49 s · with sound · 16:9

Three client types, three questions: Dino (Gen Z) wants crypto right now on his phone, Mr Kovačević (a finance man) asks who supervises Electrocoin, and Vesna is afraid of scams. The mascot Coinko has an answer for each of them, and in the end they all pay with crypto at Ivana's bakery, a PayCek merchant.

Coinko and Ana (Coinko i Ana)

37 s · with sound · 16:9

Ana's exchange closed on 1 July because it did not get a MiCA licence. Coinko shows her Electrocoin, the first MiCA licence holder in Croatia, and then convinces Marko, a petrol station owner, to accept crypto through PayCek and receive euros.

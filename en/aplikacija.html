Proposal 5 · detailed analysis

A Robinhood-style mobile app

In 2025 Robinhood had 27M funded customers and $4.47bn in revenue1. Electrocoin already has a web app, so it is not starting from scratch. The proposal is to build a mobile app for crypto first, and add shares and other products later.

  • The crypto part for which Electrocoin already holds the relevant MiCA licences: buying, selling, the wallet and the savings plan2.
  • The only extension needed for crypto: service (j), crypto transfer. With it, Electrocoin stays in the same capital class of €125,00015. HANFA decides within 40 working days of a complete application16, and Electrocoin's first application took ~5.5 months2.
  • Shares and derivatives require a separate MiFID licence with capital of €150,000 to €750,0007, or a partner that already holds one. That is the second step.

Grey areas and what is not feasible

  • Share derivatives: Robinhood calls them stock tokens, but they are not shares; they are digital tokens on a blockchain whose price tracks the share price. The token holder is not a shareholder and has no voting rights. Robinhood classifies them as derivatives, i.e. a contract between the client and itself9. Robinhood handed out OpenAI and SpaceX tokens in the EU, and OpenAI said these were not its shares17. ESMA warns that tokens without shareholder rights can mislead investors18.
  • Staking and crypto lending: MiCA does not regulate them. They are permitted only with the client's explicit consent and risk warnings5.
  • Betting on events: treated as gambling in Europe13. Not feasible.
  • Earning from routing orders to other firms: this Robinhood revenue source in the US is prohibited in the EU14. Electrocoin earns from fees, so this is not a problem.
  • Gamification: ESMA considers that techniques encouraging excessive trading are never in investors' interest19. The app should not have confetti, streak rewards or leaderboards.
  • Copy trading: automatic copying is portfolio management, which requires a licence and a suitability assessment11.

Estimate: investment, revenue and payback

All figures in this section are estimates, not data. First three years, Croatia only
  • Registrations: the default is 3% of adults in three years. Revolut today has ~22% of adults after seven years4744.
  • Deposit: the default is 25% of registered users. At eToro it is 9–10%49, but Electrocoin already has users and is well known in Croatia.
  • Turnover per user: at Robinhood ~$2,500 of crypto turnover per customer per year51. The default is €2,500.
  • Fee: the default is 1.5%, as at Bitpanda and Revolut3840.
  • Marketing cost per new user: how much marketing it takes for one user to open an account and deposit money for the first time. The default is €75, between Revolut and Robinhood321.

Registered = 3.21M adults44 × share. Users with a deposit = registered × deposit share. Turnover = users with a deposit × turnover per user. Points to watch

  • Robinhood earns most from options and interest, while crypto brings it only 7–13% of revenue511. A crypto app cannot replicate its revenue per user.
  • Competitors with large budgets are already in the market4750, so the app must offer something local: bill payments, payouts to an IBAN and support in Croatian.
  • Build cost figures come mainly from agencies and vary widely522324. Before deciding, quotes should be requested from 2–3 Croatian agencies.
